One-time setup needed

To turn your viewers' button presses into a real gamepad in your game, Windows needs a free driver called ViGEmBus. It is not installed on this PC yet.

  1. Click Download ViGEmBus below. It opens the official download page.
  2. Download the installer from the latest release and run it. Click Yes when Windows asks for permission, and restart your PC if the installer asks.
  3. Come back to this page. It continues by itself as soon as the driver is found.
